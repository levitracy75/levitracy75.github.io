Sort-a-Bean Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026, updated 15 October 2026. Published by Tracy Arcade.

Sort-a-Bean is a small bean-sorting game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. This page says what little the game keeps, what it measures about how the game is played, what the two app stores and our ad provider handle on our behalf, and how to reach us.

What the game stores

Your progress, coins, boosters, settings and purchases are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes the save. The save itself is never sent anywhere; what the game does send is described under Analytics.

Analytics

To learn which levels are too hard and where the game is unclear, Sort-a-Bean uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records how the game is played: a level started or finished (its number, how many moves and seconds it took, and whether a booster or a rescue was used), a tutorial step shown, a daily reward claimed, an ad watched, a purchase made (which product, never your payment details), and a few settings such as colorblind mode, sound and reminders. Firebase also records standard information such as the device model, operating system, app version and an approximate region worked out from your IP address.

These records are tied to a random identifier that Firebase creates for this installation of the app. They are not tied to your name, email or any account, and analytics does not collect your device's advertising identifier. We do not use analytics data for advertising. Google processes it on our behalf under its policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Uninstalling the app ends the collection and resets the identifier.

Purchases

Coins, boosters and the No Ads options are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store takes the payment and keeps your payment details; the game never sees a card number and has no way to ask for one. The store tells the game only that a purchase succeeded so it can be granted, and restores permanent purchases on a new device when you ask. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies govern that part.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for a seat in the pod, and an occasional interstitial between levels. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier and standard device information such as model, operating system and approximate location from your IP address. Google uses this under its own policy, at policies.google.com/privacy, and its advertising terms at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can limit this. On Android, Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation. On iOS, the game asks once whether it may track; saying no only makes the ads less tailored, and nothing in the game changes. Buying either No Ads product removes ads entirely.

Children

Sort-a-Bean is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game itself asks no one for any. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ad provider, contact us and we will help remove it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos or precise location from you.
  • We do not sell data.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising, and we do not use crash-reporting services.
  • We do not send push notifications. The daily reminder, if you turn it on, is set on your device by the game itself, and nothing is sent to us to make it.

Your choices

Everything the game keeps is on your device, so you control it: clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove the save. Uninstalling also stops analytics. Ad personalisation is controlled in your device settings as described above. For anything else, write to us.
