Sort-a-Bean Support

A quiet little sorting game, by Tracy Arcade.

Get in touch

Write to tracyarcade@gmail.com with the device you play on and what happened. We read everything and answer within a few days.

Purchases

Coins, boosters and No Ads are sold through the App Store or Google Play. If a purchase did not arrive, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases; permanent items come back on any device signed into the same store account. Refunds are handled by the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google at Google Play Help.

Ads

Watching an ad is always optional and only ever offered for a seat in the pod. Either No Ads product removes all ads for good.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device. There are no accounts, so it does not move between devices; uninstalling the game removes the save.

Privacy

The privacy policy is at levitracy75.github.io/privacy.html.
